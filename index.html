WHY SAKUTOOLS

Useful tools.
Private by default.

SakuTools is designed to do its work inside your browser. Your text, JSON, and generated passwords are not uploaded to a SakuTools server. No account is needed, and the core app has no analytics or advertising scripts.

For sensitive work, review the source code and your browser extensions. Offline support becomes available after the app has loaded once.
